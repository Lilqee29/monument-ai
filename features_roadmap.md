# MonumentAI Features Roadmap

## 1. Gamification & Progression (Priority)

- [x] **Dedicated Quest Hub**: A unique, immersive new page dedicated entirely to active Quests, bounties, and leaderboards to keep the map clean and make quests feel premium.
- [x] **Multiplayer Expeditions (Future)**:
  - _Group vs Group_: Squads compete to finish a city-wide scavenger hunt first.
  - _1v1 Races_: Challenge a friend to complete a 3-objective quest first.
- [x] **Quest Mode (Live Explorations)**: Users start an expedition with a visible timer. The app tracks steps and GPS routes, and assigns tasks like "Scan a bronze statue" or "Find an oak leaf."
- [x] **Animated Playback**: Upon finishing an expedition, a generated animation draws your path on the map and pops up photos at the locations you scanned.
- [x] **Achievement Badges & Titles**: Unlocking badges (e.g., "Roman Emperor" or "Night Owl") for scanning milestones or playing during specific hours.
- [x] **Daily/Weekly Quests**: Dynamic bounties like "Find a Gothic-style building today" for bonus XP.
- [x] **Levels & Passport Upgrades**: Upgrading your digital passport visuals as your level increases (e.g., Paper to Premium Leather to Elite Gold).

## 2. Advanced Tech Integrations

- [x] **Audio Tour Guides**: A button next to the "History" tab utilizing high-quality AI Text-to-Speech (like ElevenLabs) to read the monument's history aloud. The app acts as a personal tour guide.
- [x] **Geofencing Notifications**: The app runs silently in the background. If a user walks within 500 meters of an unvisited historical site, a push notification triggers: "🚨 Rare Monument Nearby! Open your camera!"
- **Live AR (Augmented Reality) Radar**: Floating digital pins overlayed on the phone's camera view pointing toward unvisited landmarks.

## 3. Social & Community

- **Explorer's Feed**: A social feed where you can see photos your friends have captured and their associated histories.
- **Co-Op Discoveries**: Link up with friends for multiplier bonuses when scanning difficult monuments together.
- **"Who's Here?" Feature**: Opt-in map feature to see if other explorers are nearby.

## 4. Offline & Utility

- **Offline "Travel" Mode**: Pre-downloaded map regions and a lightweight local AI model for scanning without a data connection.
- **Itinerary Builder**: Users can tap on untraced map sites to add them to a "Weekend Trip" with generated walking routes.
